THE RETREAT
“A road trip into the heart of nature’s artistry—where cascading waterfalls meet lush tea landscapes, moonlit bonfires, and crisp countryside air.”

Located along Ikuu–Chera Road in Chuka, Tharaka Nithi County, The STER Farmhouse was created by hosts Stella and Eric as a sanctuary away from the rapid pace of the city. Here, days start with birds chirping along the old Safari Rally trail and finish on an expansive viewing balcony as the sun sets over rolling tea hills.

3 Private Bedrooms • Sleeps Up to 6 • 1.5-Acre Farm • Sunset Balcony • Bonfire Backyard • 3 Hours from Nairobi

THE 1.5-ACRE LIVING FARM

From Soil to Table

STER Farmhouse is an active, integrated ecological farm. The grounds feature organic kitchen gardens bursting with seasonal leafy greens, tomatoes, and fresh herbs, complemented by a flourishing fruit orchard.

Children and adults alike will love interacting with our farm animals—including poultry, dairy goats, and friendly rabbit rearing. Guests are welcome to harvest fresh organic produce at subsidized rates for their cooking.

• ORGANIC GREENS • DAIRY GOATS • TEA TRAILS • FRESH PRODUCE
AGRO-TOURISM & COUNTRYSIDE LIVING

STER Integrated Hass Avocado Farm

Looking for something truly authentic during your stay at STER Farmhouse? Take a relaxed 30-minute scenic drive to STER Farms for an immersive, behind-the-scenes look at Kenyan highland farming.

Wander through our flourishing Hass avocado and banana groves, visit the active beehives producing pure highland honey, and meet the free-range goats and chickens. It’s a wonderful chance to reconnect with nature, learn sustainable farming, and simply enjoy the slower, richer side of life in Tharaka Nithi.

QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

Frequently Asked Questions

How many guests can stay at The STER Farmhouse? +
The farmhouse sleeps up to 6 guests comfortably across private, thoughtfully appointed bedrooms (including the Twin Room and Tea View Room) with 2 well-appointed bathrooms.
Is the stay self-catering, or is a chef available? +
The house is fully self-catering with a complete kitchen including a stove, refrigerator, microwave, and cooking essentials. If you prefer to relax completely without cooking, a personal chef is available upon advance request for a small additional fee.
Are pets and children allowed? +
Yes! We are proud to be pet friendly and family friendly. Our 1.5-acre property provides an expansive, secure green lawn where dogs and kids can play freely.
Can we visit Ngoko Waterfall and the Mutunguruni Giant Tree? +
Absolutely. Ngoko Waterfall is a 20-minute scenic drive away, and the 85-meter Mutunguruni tree is approximately 12 minutes away. We can connect you with a friendly local guide for hiking and waterfall swimming excursions.
What are the check-in and check-out times? +
Standard check-in is from 2:00 PM, and check-out is by 11:00 AM. Flexible timings can often be accommodated depending on adjacent guest bookings.
How do payment and cancellation policies work? +
A 50% deposit via M-Pesa or Bank transfer confirms and locks your dates. The remaining balance is payable prior to check-in. Cancellations made at least 7 days before check-in receive a full refund or date rescheduling.
